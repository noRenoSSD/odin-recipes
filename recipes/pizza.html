Pizza

Description

You can make a delicious homemade pizza using simple pantry ingredients and a hot oven.

Ingredients

Pizza Dough

  • 1 1/2 cups warm water (105°F–115°F)
  • 1 packet (2 1/4 teaspoons) active dry or instant yeast
  • 3 3/4 cups bread flour (or all-purpose flour)
  • 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
  • 2 teaspoons salt
  • 1 teaspoon sugar

Sauce and Toppings

  • 1/2 cup tomato sauce or pizza sauce
  • 2 cups shredded or sliced mozzarella cheese
  • 1/4 cup grated Parmesan cheese (optional)
  • Your favorite toppings (pepperoni, mushrooms, fresh basil)

Instructions

Make the Dough

  1. Make the Dough
    • Mix the warm water and sugar in a large bowl, then sprinkle the yeast over the top.
    • Let it sit for 5 minutes until it gets foamy.
    • Add the flour, olive oil, and salt.
    • Knead the mixture by hand or with a mixer for 7 to 10 minutes until smooth and slightly sticky.
    • Shape the dough into a ball, place it in a lightly oiled bowl, and turn it once to coat.
    • Cover the bowl with plastic wrap or a clean kitchen towel.
    • Let the dough rise in a warm place for 1 to 1.5 hours, or until it has doubled in size.
  2. Let It Rise
    • Coat a clean bowl with a little olive oil and place the dough inside, turning it to coat.
    • Cover the bowl and let the dough rise in a warm spot for about 1.5 hours (or put it in the fridge for 24 hours for better flavor).
  3. Assemble and Bake
    • Preheat your oven to a very high heat, around 450°F–475°F.
    • Punch down the dough and stretch it out onto a greased baking pan or a peel dusted with cornmeal.
    • Spread the tomato sauce over the dough, leaving a small border for the crust.
    • Add the mozzarella cheese and your preferred toppings.
    • Bake for 12 to 15 minutes until the crust is golden and the cheese is bubbly.
