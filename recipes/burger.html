Burger

Description

Make a classic, juicy homemade cheeseburger with simple ingredients and an easy stovetop or grill method.

Ingredients

  • Ground beef: 1 pound (450g), preferably an 80/20 blend for juiciness.
  • Seasoning: 1 tablespoon Worcestershire sauce, ½ teaspoon garlic powder, ½ teaspoon onion powder, ¾ teaspoon salt, and ¼ teaspoon black pepper.
  • Buns: 4 brioche or soft hamburger buns, halved.
  • Cheese: 4 slices of American or Cheddar cheese.
  • Toppings: Leaf lettuce, sliced tomatoes, sliced red onion, and pickles.
  • Condiments: Ketchup, mayonnaise, or mustard.

Steps

  1. Mix the beef: Gently combine the ground beef, Worcestershire sauce, garlic powder, onion powder, and black pepper in a large bowl. Do not overmix, or the meat will get tough.
  2. Shape the patties: Divide the mixture into 4 equal portions and form patties about ½-inch thick. Make them slightly wider than your buns because they shrink when cooked. Press a small dent in the center of each patty with your thumb so they stay flat while cooking.
  3. Sprinkle both sides of the patties evenly with salt.
  4. Cook: Heat a skillet or grill over medium-high heat with a little oil. Cook the patties for 3 to 4 minutes per side until nicely browned. Add a slice of cheese during the last minute of cooking and cover to melt.
  5. Place the bun halves cut-side down on the hot pan for 1 minute until golden.
  6. Assemble: Spread condiments on the buns. Layer lettuce, tomato, the cheesy burger patty, and onion. Top with the upper bun and serve.
